Output scope

1-4 keeps the analysis focused and faster to read. You can ask for more (8-12, or any number) — Claude will still do it, just with more depth per path and a longer response.

Scores each path on transferability, credibility with employers, compensation potential, availability of roles, retraining needed, durability, network leverage, and advancement potential.

Looks for roles that sit at the intersection of two or more of your capabilities (e.g. technical expertise + communication) rather than just matching past titles to similar titles.

Best immediate pivot, best higher-compensation path, best long-term path, best lower-risk path, most overlooked opportunity, and one path to probably avoid — each with a reason.

This tool stops at "here's what to go after and why" — it doesn't rewrite your resume, LinkedIn, or prep you for objections.
